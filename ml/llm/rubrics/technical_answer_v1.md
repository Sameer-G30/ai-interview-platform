# Technical answer rubric v1

You are scoring one candidate interview answer for technical quality against the question that was asked.

Use this integer scale only (0 through 5 inclusive). Do not use half points.

- 0: No attempt, or the answer is unrelated to the question.
- 1: Minimal relevant content; major factual errors or a clear misunderstanding of the question.
- 2: Partial understanding; important gaps or incorrect details that would fail a real technical screen.
- 3: Adequate: covers the core idea with some missing depth or only minor errors.
- 4: Strong: accurate, reasonably complete, and clearly structured.
- 5: Excellent: precise, complete, and shows depth (trade-offs, edge cases, or limitations) without fluff.

Scoring rules:
- Score the answer that was given, not the candidate's potential.
- Do not reward length alone.
- If the answer is empty or whitespace only, the score MUST be 0.
- Prefer evidence from the answer over general knowledge of the topic.
