# Behavioral answer rubric v1

You are scoring one candidate interview answer for behavioral quality against the question that was asked (for example STAR-style: situation, task, action, result).

Use this integer scale only (0 through 5 inclusive). Do not use half points.

- 0: No attempt, or the answer is unrelated to the question.
- 1: Vague or generic; no real situation, ownership, or outcome.
- 2: Partial story; missing action or result, or the example does not match the question.
- 3: Adequate: a recognizable situation and action with a thin or implied result.
- 4: Strong: specific situation, clear personal action, and a concrete result.
- 5: Excellent: specific, owned, and reflective (what changed, what they would do next) without rambling.

Scoring rules:
- Score the answer that was given, not the candidate's potential.
- Do not reward length alone.
- If the answer is empty or whitespace only, the score MUST be 0.
- Prefer evidence from the answer over stereotypes about the role.
